# 05. UX

## Overview

The best UI does more than look good—it communicates clearly and helps users complete actions smoothly.

## UX patterns to master

- press feedback
- loading states
- transition feedback
- success confirmation
- bottom sheet interactions
- onboarding flows
- drag, swipe, and scroll interactions

## Good animation practice

- motion should support the action
- timing should feel natural
- animation should be consistent across the app
- avoid visual clutter
- support reduced motion preferences

## Example micro-interaction ideas

- card lift on tap
- button pulse on click
- list item slide-in on render
- toast and snackbar transitions
- toolbar collapse on scroll
- checkmark success after save

## UX checklist

- Is the layout easy to scan?
- Is the hierarchy obvious?
- Is feedback immediate?
- Is the app responsive and smooth?
- Does the design feel premium without being distracting?

## Final note

Great UX is a mix of structure, clarity, motion, and consistency. Animation should guide the user, not take over the screen.
